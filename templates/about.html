Our Mission & Vision

We are a family of imperfect people pursuing a perfect God—seeking real life, real love, and real freedom in Jesus. Our vision is simple: make disciples, serve Russellville, and reflect Christ in everyday life.

Mission

Lead people from curiosity to commitment through authentic worship, biblical teaching, and practical service.

Vision

A beacon in Russellville where families heal, faith grows, and the next generation learns to walk with God.

Pastor’s Story

Pastor Mario loves this city. His calling is to serve people where they are—no polish required. From living-room prayers to Sunday gatherings, his heartbeat is the same: point people to Jesus and walk with them.

Core Beliefs

  • God

    One God who reveals Himself to us—holy, loving, and faithful.

  • Scripture

    The Bible is our final authority for faith and life.

  • Jesus

    Salvation is found in Jesus Christ; we are saved by grace through faith.

  • The Church

    We gather to worship, grow, and serve—sent to love our city.
